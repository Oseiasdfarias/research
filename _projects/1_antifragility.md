Robust and resilient systems aim to keep performance under disturbances and faults. Antifragility goes further: it aims at performance gains under stress.

At UFABC, in a project funded by the U.S. Air Force Office of Scientific Research (AFOSR), I study how to apply this idea to power converters. The work uses a Buck converter as its plant, with model-based residual generation, CUSUM fault detection, and controller reconfiguration.
