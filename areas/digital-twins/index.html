Digital twins and virtual labs for engineering education

A low-cost physical aeropendulum and a digital twin for teaching control.

Control courses often stay on paper, without a real, low-cost plant for testing closed-loop control laws. The Virtual Lab is an open platform that combines an ESP32 aeropendulum, a PID controller running on the board, an interface for real-time tests, and a 3D digital twin that follows the measured motion.
