I work on control systems that detect faults and reconfigure themselves, combining multi-agent supervision, predictive control and system identification for power converters.

I am pursuing two master's degrees. At [UFABC](https://www.ufabc.edu.br/), in Electrical and Information Engineering, I research artificial antifragility in a project funded by the U.S. Air Force Office of Scientific Research (AFOSR). At [UFPA](https://www.ufpa.br/), in Applied Computing, I study predictive control and deep reinforcement learning for power electronics.

I also build open hardware and digital twins for engineering education, and I maintain [Synapsys](https://github.com/synapsys-lab/synapsys), a Python framework that joins control systems and machine learning. My engineering projects are in my [portfolio](https://oseiasdfarias.github.io/).
